The Mao Students' Union (MSU) Martyrs' Trophy 2026 inaugural programme took place on Monday, September 21, 2026, at the Asufii Football Academy (AFA) Turf Ground in Asufii, Punanamei, Mao. Organized by the Mao Students' Union in collaboration with the Mao Football Association under the theme "Honouring our Martyrs, Building our Future," the ceremony marked the kickoff of the 16-team football tournament.

The **Inaugural Function** for the **Martyrs' Trophy 2026** (organised by the **Mao Students' Union**) took place on **21st September 2026** at **11:00 AM**. **Lt. Gen. (Retd.) VS Atem**, Vice Chairperson of the Advisory and Executive Council (VC, AEM) and Hon'ble Deputy Ato Kilonser, attended as Chief Guest, with **Chokhoni Charakho** and **L Adani** serving as comperes.

The opening proceedings featured the unveiling of the monolith by the Chief Guest, its dedication by **Rev. Kh. Manikho Robert** (Finance & Property Secretary, MBCA), and flag hoisting. The formal programme began with an invocation by **Rev. Fr. Athili Lokho** (Rector & Parish Priest, St. Mary's Parish, Punanamei), a prelude by the **Kalinamai Women Society**, a welcome address by MSU President **Shuli Chakre**, and the concept note and cup presentation by Organising Committee Convenor **Kapani Lorho**, followed by a moment of silence in tribute to the martyrs.

The programme continued with an introduction and presentation by Organising Committee Co-Convenor **Pfokrehrii Matekho Pekosii**, a solo item (_"Melodious Voice"_) by **Rachel Aruo**, greetings from the **All Naga Students' Association, Manipur (ANSAM)**, and a performance (_"Go ye into all the world"_) by **Mount Gibeon Hr. Sec. School, Tadubi**. It concluded with the Chief Guest's speech, oath taking led by the Chief Referee of the **Mao Football Association**, and the formal declaration of the Martyrs' Trophy open.

The **Closing Ceremony** took place on **6th October 2026**, starting at **11:00 AM**, with **Eno Dr. L. Dikho**, Hon'ble Deputy Chief Minister of Manipur, attending as Chief Guest and Kholi Eloziini serving as compere. The programme began with a performance of _"Heal the World"_ by Okai Academy, Mao, followed by a welcome note and presentation from MSU General Secretary Lot Pfote, greetings from the Naga Students' Federation (NSF), a musical item (_"Harmonious Tune"_) by Kayiso Kayina, and the Chief Guest's speech before the grand finale kick-off.

Following the final match, the **Prize Distribution Ceremony** was hosted by MSU Vice President Tuni Khrasi as MC, with a vote of thanks by Organising Committee Member Secretary D. Thohrii and a closing prayer by Pastor A. Neli of MBC Chowainu. On the pitch, **Song Song FC** clinched the title with a 4–0 victory over Pudunamei FC in the final, while Chowainamei Khullen FC received the Fair Play award.
