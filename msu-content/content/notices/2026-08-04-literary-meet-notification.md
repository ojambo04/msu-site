## Official Notification

This notice outlines the schedule, registration details, category rules, and venue guidelines for the **Inter-School Literary Meet 2026** to be held on August 14, 2026 at Mao Town Hall, Senapati.

### Registration Details

All participating schools must submit their entries before August 10, 2026. Each school may field a maximum of three participants per category.

### Categories

- Debate (English & Mao)
- Essay Writing (English)
- Extempore Speech (English & Mao)

### Venue Guidelines

Participants are requested to report at the venue by 9:30 AM. Entry passes will be verified at the gate.
