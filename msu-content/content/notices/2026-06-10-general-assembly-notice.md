## General Assembly Meeting Notice

All unit presidents and secretaries of the Mao Students' Union constituent units are hereby notified of the **Bi-Annual General Assembly** to be held on September 10, 2026 at the Central HQ, Tadubi.

### Agenda

1. Review of the previous assembly's resolutions
2. Discussion on the upcoming academic year's programs
3. Financial report presentation
4. Election of sub-committee members

Attendance is **mandatory** for all constituent unit representatives. Units failing to send representatives will be noted in the official minutes.
