## Press Statement

**Date:** May 22, 2026

The Mao Students' Union (MSU) strongly condemns the recent arbitrary cuts to the educational budget that have directly impacted thousands of students across the region.

We stand in unwavering solidarity with all affected student bodies and urge the concerned authorities to immediately review and reverse these decisions.

Education is a fundamental right, and any act that undermines access to quality education is an act against the future of our community.

The MSU calls upon all member units to remain united and to participate in peaceful, democratic means of protest to make our voices heard.

*Issued by the Office of the General Secretary, Mao Students' Union.*
