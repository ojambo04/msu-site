The Mao Students' Union (MSU) is one of the oldest and most respected student body organizations, founded in 1933 with the enduring motto, "Sacrifice and Progress." For over nine decades, the Union has remained committed to serving the interests of the student community while fostering unity, leadership, and social responsibility.

The Union comprises 27 constituent units and 4 subordinate bodies, working collectively to strengthen student representation and promote inclusive development. Through coordinated efforts, the MSU addresses educational concerns, advocates for students' welfare, and encourages active participation in academic, social, and community affairs.

Beyond student advocacy, the Mao Students' Union plays a vital role in preserving and promoting the rich cultural heritage, traditions, and identity of the Mao community. The Union is equally dedicated to environmental conservation, organizing initiatives that encourage sustainable practices, ecological awareness, and responsible stewardship of natural resources.

Guided by its founding principles of sacrifice, service, and progress, the Mao Students' Union continues to inspire generations of students to uphold excellence, integrity, and a strong sense of community, while contributing meaningfully to society.
